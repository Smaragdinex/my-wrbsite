Privacy Policy

Wooden Rails · last updated 14 August 2026

Wooden Rails has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of our own. We do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. The game runs entirely on your device and works with no network connection at all.

What is stored on your device

The game saves three things locally so it can pick up where you left off:

  • which levels you have unlocked
  • your chosen language
  • whether sound is muted

This never leaves your device, except that on some platforms it may be backed up by the platform itself (for example iCloud backup, or the CrazyGames account system on the web version) so your progress follows you between devices. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising

The iOS app shows ads through Google AdMob: a rewarded ad if you choose to watch one for a hint, and an occasional ad between levels. Google's SDK — not us — may collect the following in order to serve and measure those ads:

DataWhat Google says it is used for
Device identifier (IDFA)Third-party advertising and analytics
Advertising dataWhich ads were shown and interacted with
Product interactionApp launches and taps, to measure ad performance
Crash and performance dataDiagnosing problems, and ad performance
IP addressEstimating approximate location and preventing fraud

On first launch iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalised ads. The game plays exactly the same either way — nothing is locked behind that choice. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Wooden Rails is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no way to contact other players.

The web version

The version at xarts.games/wooden-rails shows no ads and loads no third-party scripts. It stores the same three local settings in your browser. When played on CrazyGames, that platform's own privacy policy applies to anything it collects.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
