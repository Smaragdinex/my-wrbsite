Wooden Rails — Support

Build the track, then watch the train go.

Something broken, or stuck on a level? Write to contact@xarts.games and we will reply. It helps if you say which level number and which device.

How to play

  1. Tap a track piece in the column on the left to pick it up.
  2. Tap a square on the mat to place it.
  3. Tap a piece you have already placed to rotate it.
  4. Pick the eraser to take a piece back, or the bin to clear the whole mat.
  5. When the track joins the two sheds, tap GO.

Passengers standing on the mat must be driven over before the train reaches the station. You get more pieces than you strictly need, so a roundabout route still works — but the fewer pieces you use, the more stars you earn. Three stars means you found the shortest route there is.

Questions

I am stuck on a level. Is there a hint?

Yes — the 💡 button. It watches a short ad and then places one correct piece for you. Take as many as you like; there is no limit. Each piece it places is locked so it cannot be cleared by accident. If the ad fails to load you still get the hint.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on your device, so it survives closing the app. Deleting the app removes it. On the web version played through CrazyGames, progress is tied to your CrazyGames account and follows you between devices.

How do I change the language?

The button under Levels on the title screen. Eight languages: English, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Italiano, Español and العربية. The game picks your device language the first time and remembers what you choose after that.

How do I turn the sound off?

The speaker button on the right edge during play. It mutes both the music and the sound effects, and it is remembered.

Why does it ask about tracking when I first open it?

That prompt is from iOS, and it only decides whether the ads you see are personalised. Declining changes nothing about the game — every level, hint and feature works exactly the same. See the privacy policy for what the ad SDK collects.

Why is the app landscape only?

The play mat is wider than it is tall. In portrait it would be cropped on both sides, or shrunk until the track pieces were too small to tap accurately. The iPad version works in any orientation.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The whole game is inside the app — put the device in airplane mode and all 50 levels still play. A connection is only used to fetch an ad if you ask for a hint.

Can I play without installing anything?

Yes — play it in a browser. Same 50 levels, no ads, nothing to install.

Reporting a problem

Email contact@xarts.games. Please include:

  1. the level number
  2. your device and iOS version
  3. what you expected to happen, and what happened instead

A screenshot is worth a lot — especially for anything that looks wrong on screen.
